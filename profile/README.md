# Elapse

**A native macOS app for organizing and managing your time.**

Elapse turns the hours you actually work into clean, organized records — with almost no manual bookkeeping. It runs as both a main window and a menu-bar app, tracks the apps and windows you use automatically, lays your day out on a visual color-coded timeline, and can push tracked time straight to Jira issues as worklogs. Your data stays on your Mac.

![Activities View](app-activities.png)

## Highlights

- **Automatic activity tracking** — Elapse records the apps and windows you use, so your day is already mapped before you touch anything.
- **Visual timeline** — see the day laid out as a color-coded timeline above your time entries.
- **Scheduled timers & presets** — plan work ahead and start recurring tasks in one click.
- **Jira worklogs** — push tracked time straight to Jira issues, with reminders so nothing slips.
- **Calendar overlay** — see your calendar events alongside what you actually worked on.
- **Private by default** — your data stays on your Mac.
